Pure by nature

Gayatri Herbals Pvt. Ltd. is a manufacturer of herbal products, hair care products and natural cosmetics based in Thane, Maharashtra. Every formulation blends time-tested Ayurvedic wisdom with modern science, so nature can work harder for you.

From aloe vera gels, shampoos and serums to balms, powders and toothpaste — along with complete cosmetic third party manufacturing services — we make pure, effective herbal care accessible to everyone.
